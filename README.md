# Ocean

A vision for a persistent, sovereign, self-improving computing environment organized around people, their information, and their intentions.

Read the complete [Ocean vision](VISION.md), including its architecture, references, and prior art.

## See what using Ocean could feel like

Two short, illustrated walkthroughs show how an agent could compose a tool that you can adjust and use again:

- [Plan dinners for the week](examples/weekly-dinners.md) — plan on your computer, adjust on a tablet, shop on your phone, and cook with your watch.
- [Run a neighborhood repair day](examples/repair-day.md) — follow the same repair ticket from check-in to completion across all four devices.

These are proposed experiences with generated UI mockups.
